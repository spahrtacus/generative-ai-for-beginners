## 1. Style guide

### Look
- **Era:** Chicago, 1855–1897 (plus a short modern section at the end)
- **Palette:** warm tungsten interiors, cold blue-grey exteriors, coal-smoke haze. Red brick is the hero color of the town.
- **Lens:** 35mm anamorphic, shallow depth of field, slight film grain, gentle halation on lights
- **Mood shift:** Acts 1–2 are warm, golden and orderly. Act 3 turns colder. Act 4 is fire-orange and black. The close goes back to soft daylight.
- **Aspect:** 16:9, at least 2K

### Prompt prefix (paste in front of EVERY still)
```
Cinematic still from a historical documentary, Chicago 1880s-1890s, photorealistic,
35mm anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, soft film grain, period-accurate clothing
and architecture, natural motivated lighting, muted desaturated palette with warm
tungsten accents, coal-smoke haze, no text, no logos, no modern objects —
```

### Negative prompt (when the model supports it)
```
text, watermark, logo, modern clothing, cars, power lines, plastic, smiling at camera,
cartoon, illustration, oversaturated, distorted hands, extra fingers
```

### Rules
1. **No AI faces of real people.** Pullman, Debs, Cleveland, Robert Todd Lincoln and Randolph appear as archival photos only.
2. **Anonymous people stay anonymous.** Use backs to camera, silhouettes, mid-distance or hands. That avoids faces drifting between frames and the uncanny look of AI faces.
3. **No gore.** Act 4 violence is shown through fire, smoke and silhouettes.
4. **Recurring set pieces stay consistent:** the red-brick rowhouse street (S2-02) and the cemetery (S0-01). Generate these first, then pass them as image references to the shots that reuse them.
